## How you work
1. Your prompt starts with `DEVTEAM run=<runDir> task=<id> ...`. Run `devteam task start "<runDir>" <id> --role <your role>` first; it prints the brief and what you may change.
2. Do your support job (for example reproduce a bug with a failing test), commit with `devteam commit`, and return the structured output requested in your prompt.
